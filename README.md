## How I work

- **Measure before scaling.** I define the baseline and success measures before launch, then separate credible impact from coincidence.
- **Keep people accountable.** Model output is evidence for a reviewer—not the final decision. Every workflow includes a human review point.
- **Protect sensitive information.** I design for data minimization and can incorporate private or local models when text cannot leave the organization’s environment.
- **End with a decision.** Each engagement produces a plain-language recommendation, its limitations, and the next evidence needed.
